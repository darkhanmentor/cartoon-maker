🎬 Cartoon Maker

Apni cartoon kahani banayein — characters, scenes, Urdu voiceover aur video!

✏️ Haath se banaye hue cartoon — koi ready-made tasveer nahi
Ye tool kya karta hai: Aap ki kahani se simple 2D cartoon video banata hai — original characters, rang-birange scenes, Urdu bolna (voiceover) aur halki harkat. Bilkul Chunnu Khargosh / Lily wali videos jaisa.
Imandari wali baat: Ye TV studio jaisi full animation (daurna, ladna, munh hila kar bolna) NAHI banata — wo bade studio ka kaam hai. Ye simple, pyara cartoon banata hai jo bachon ko pasand aata hai.
Original characters: Motu Patlu ke naam, shakal ya kahani copy nahi karte — wo copyrighted hain. Hamare characters apne hain (Golu, Chintu aur dost), un jaisi comedy zaroor hai!
Haath se banaya gaya: Saare characters aur scenes haath se banaye hue (hand-drawn) hain — koi ready-made tasveer istemal nahi hoti. Har character ki apni awaaz aur apni chaal hai, jaise asli cartoon mein hota hai — Golu bhaari awaaz mein dheere bolta hai, Chintu tez aur unchi awaaz mein, aur bacha uchhal-uchhal kar chalta hai.
Asal usool — ORIGINAL NATURAL: Sab kuch original (characters, kahaniyan, art — kisi ki naqal nahi) aur sab kuch natural (haath se banaya hua, insaan jaisi awaaz aur chaal — machine jaisa nahi).

✨ Prompt se Video — likhein, poora project khud ban jaye

Apni kahani ka prompt yahan likhein (Urdu ya English) — masalan: "Do dost gaon mein aam churate hain, phir maafi maangte hain." Tool prompt ko parh kar poora video project bana dega: characters (apni awaaz + movement ke saath), scenes (har scene alag), dialogues, backgrounds, props, title, ending aur lambi kahani ho to movie ke parts bhi. Ye koi AI nahi — ek smart builder hai jo aap ke lafzon aur jumlon se project banata hai. Banne ke baad aap har cheez edit kar sakte hain.

Lambi kahani (8 ya zyada jumle) ho to Movie mode khud ban jayega — title card, 3 parts, climax aur ending ke saath.

📖 1 — Kahani (Story)

⏱ Kul andaza duration: —

Sample dabayein to tayyar kahani load ho jayegi — phir neeche scenes mein tabdeeli kar sakte hain. Scenes ki koi limit nahi — 1 minute ki video ho ya 20 minute ki film, jitne scenes chahiye add karein.

🎥 Movie Structure (Film ki banawat)

Movie mein title card (shuru mein), acts (hissay), climax aur end credits (aakhir mein) khud lag jate hain. Har scene ko neeche us ke act mein daalein.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 2 — Characters (Kirdar)

Kisi character par tap karein to wo scene mein add hoga. Naam badalna ho to neeche likh dein — naam har jagah update ho jayega. Har character ki apni awaaz aur apni chaal hai — pitch aur speed ke slider se awaaz badlein, aur 🔊 dabakar sunain.

🎭 3 — Scenes (Manzar)

Har scene mein: background chunein, 1–2 characters lagayein, un ka expression chunein, aur dialogue likhein.

▶️ 4 — Preview aur Video

—
Preview chalane ke liye neeche Play dabayein.

Subtitles har dialogue ki line ke saath khud lag jate hain — awaaz ke saath sync mein, Urdu mein. Video export karte waqt .srt file bhi ban jati hai (YouTube par video ke saath alag se upload kar sakte hain).

Awaaz (voiceover) check ho rahi hai…

Export ke baare mein sach: Download hone wali video mein awaaz (audio) nahi hogi — sirf tasveer + likha hua dialogue hoga. Browser ki awaaz ko video mein record karna mumkin nahi. Awaaz ke liye Preview mein sunain, ya apni recording alag se video par lagayein.

Lambi video / movie ke baare mein sach: Export ke waqt ye tab khula rakhna zaroori hai — band kar diya to video adhoori reh jayegi. Lambi film mein phone ki memory zyada istemal hoti hai (lagbhag 350 MB har 10 minute ke liye). Agar phone par mushkil ho to movie ko act-wise export karein — har act ki alag video ban jayegi, phir unhein jod lein.

🌙 Islamic Kahaniyan — Ahem Baat

Islamic / akhlaqi kahaniyon mein hum sirf original characters (bachay, ustaad, ammi, dost) dikhate hain. Ambiya (Prophets) aur Sahaba ki tasveer bilkul nahi banate — ye hamari izzat aur ehtiram ka tareeqa hai. Kahaniyan sach, imaandari, mehnat, walidain ki izzat aur achay akhlaaq par honi chahiye.
